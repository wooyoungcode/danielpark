Mechanical engineering, with artificial intelligence built in rather than bolted on.
01
Statement

I want to be the engineer who is fluent in AI.

My reasoning is simple. Within a few years AI will be embedded in nearly every field, and the people who actually move those fields forward will not be the ones who only understand AI, or only understand the field. They will be the domain experts who know AI well enough to bend it toward a real problem.

A physician who can build with a model. An engineer who can put intelligence inside a mechanism instead of next to it. That is the person I am training to become.

Mechanical engineering is the foundation I trust: loads, tolerances, materials, and a physical world that does not care what you intended. It is unforgiving in a way I find clarifying. AI is the layer I am adding on top of that, deliberately, while the foundation is still being poured.

Everything below is practice for that. The competitions taught me to iterate under a deadline. Basketball taught me to keep showing up. The orchestra taught me that precision is a form of respect.
