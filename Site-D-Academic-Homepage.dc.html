Hi.

Daniel Park

Aspiring mechanical engineer, applied AI

I am a Grade 11 student at Fayston Preparatory School, Suji Campus, graduating in 2028. I plan to study mechanical engineering and to build a working command of artificial intelligence alongside it, rather than after it.

My reasoning is simple. Within a few years AI will be embedded in nearly every field, and the people who move those fields forward will be the domain experts who know AI well enough to bend it toward a real problem. A physician who can build with a model. An engineer who can put intelligence inside a mechanism instead of next to it.

Mechanical engineering is unforgiving in a way I find clarifying. Loads, tolerances and materials do not care what you intended, and that is exactly why I trust it as a foundation.
